Strategy

Think first. Build smart.

Strategy studies the game and helps the team decide what the robot should do before we start designing or building.

  • Analyze the game rules and scoring opportunities
  • Compare possible robot strategies and tradeoffs
  • Research successful FTC teams and match play
  • Create robot priorities and requirements
  • Help guide design decisions using data
Game analysisRulesDataDecision making

Design

From ideas to blueprints.

Design turns strategy into robot concepts, sketches, CAD models, prototypes, and plans the team can actually build.

  • Create robot concepts and mechanism ideas
  • Learn CAD and build digital models
  • Plan prototypes and design experiments
  • Think through packaging and robot layout
  • Lead design reviews and improve ideas
CADMechanismsCreativityTradeoffs

Build

Make it real.

Build creates the physical robot: parts, assemblies, wiring, sensors, repairs, testing, and all the hands-on work.

  • Manufacture, assemble, and repair robot parts
  • Wire motors, sensors, hubs, and batteries
  • Use tools, 3D printers, and hardware systems
  • Test prototypes and improve reliability
  • Keep the robot competition-ready
Tools3D printingWiringTesting

Code

Bring the robot to life.

Code writes the software that makes the robot drive, aim, sense, think, and perform in autonomous and driver-controlled modes.

  • Program the robot using Java and the FTC SDK
  • Create TeleOp controls and driver assists
  • Develop autonomous routines and path following
  • Experiment with sensors, vision, and telemetry
  • Debug and improve robot performance
JavaAutonomousVisionDebugging

Outreach

Grow the team. Grow the community.

Outreach shares our team story, supports sponsors, mentors younger students, and helps Evergreen Dynamics make an impact beyond the robot.

  • Plan community outreach and STEM events
  • Support sponsor communication and thank-yous
  • Mentor younger robotics teams
  • Create presentations and demonstrations
  • Track team impact and achievements
LeadershipSpeakingMentoringEvents

Drive

Practice. Perform. Compete.

Drive prepares the team for competition by mastering the robot, practicing under pressure, scouting, and executing match strategy.

  • Practice driving, operating, and human player skills
  • Develop match strategy and competition routines
  • Scout other teams and study match performance
  • Communicate with alliance partners
  • Help the team perform under pressure
PracticeScoutingPressureTeamwork
