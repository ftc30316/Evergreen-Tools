Student Challenge

Name That Method!

Work with your teammates to complete the challenge. Keep your action secret.

1

Pick a secret action

Choose something you know how to do. Do not tell anyone what the action is.

2

Describe it

Explain your action in 5–10 steps. Be specific, but do not say the name of the action.

3

Name that action

Another student listens to your steps and gives the whole action one short name.

4

Turn it into code

Convert the action into a Java-style method name. Example: Tie your shoes → tieShoes()

Ideas to get you started
• Tie your shoes • Make a PB&J sandwich • Shoot a basketball • Brush your teeth • Wrap a birthday present • Make a paper airplane • Make a bowl of cereal • Take a picture with your phone

ABSTRACTION

Abstraction means hiding unnecessary details behind a simple, useful way to interact with something.

Many detailed steps
→
oneSimpleName()

Without abstraction

intakeMotor.setPower(1);
intakeServo.setPosition(0.72);
distanceSensor.getDistance(...);
led.setPattern(...);
// and more...
→

With abstraction

intake.collect();
The OpMode says WHAT the robot should do.
The subsystem knows HOW to do it.
Why this matters in FTC: If the robot design changes—for example, the intake goes from one motor to two—the OpMode can still call intake.collect(). Only the Intake subsystem needs to change.
