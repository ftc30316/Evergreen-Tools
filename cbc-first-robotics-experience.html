A gateway into robotics

Students can experience robotics today.

The FIRST Robotics Experience introduces students, families, and educators to real youth robotics programs available throughout the Richland, Pasco, and Kennewick area.

Visitors explore simplified versions of FIRST LEGO League and FIRST Tech Challenge activities, interact with robot fields and displays, and learn how to join or start local robotics teams.

“The goal is for every student to leave thinking: I could build that. I could code that. I could join a team.”

Explore the experience

A hands-on robotics destination inside the STEM Center.

Each exhibit zone is designed to be approachable, memorable, and directly connected to real robotics programs students can join.

FIRST LEGO League Zone

Students drive LEGO robots, run simplified missions on the current season's challenge table, and try beginner-friendly coding activities.

LEGO robot missions Block coding Team innovation

FIRST Tech Challenge Zone

Visitors operate metal competition-style robots on a modified FTC field, score game pieces, and learn how software, sensors, and strategy work together.

Driver challenge Java coding Computer vision

Engineering Design Studio

Interactive stations let students experiment with gears, robot arms, sensors, wheels, linkages, and mechanical tradeoffs.

What visitors can do

Interactive activities designed for school field trips.

Activities can be experienced individually during public visits or as rotating stations during school group visits.

🤖

Drive a Robot

Operate a simplified competition robot and score points on a student-friendly field challenge.

💻

Code a Mission

Use beginner programming activities to control robot movement, sensors, and autonomous actions.

⚙️

Test Engineering Ideas

Explore speed, torque, traction, mechanisms, and design choices through hands-on experiments.

👁️

See Robot Vision

Watch cameras, AprilTags, and object detection help robots understand the world around them.

🏆

Follow the Competition

View videos, scoring examples, trophies, and student stories from local robotics teams.

📍

Find a Team

Use a local team finder to connect students, parents, teachers, and mentors with robotics opportunities.

For educators

A STEM field trip students will remember.

The FIRST Robotics Experience supports classroom learning by connecting science, technology, engineering, math, communication, creativity, and career awareness.

  • Designed for middle school and high school audiences
  • Supports STEM, CTE, career exploration, and enrichment goals
  • Works for classrooms, clubs, homeschool groups, and youth organizations
  • Connects students to local teams and ongoing participation
