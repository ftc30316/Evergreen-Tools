4 Guidelines & Roles

Members

Members can:

  • Read announcements and updates
  • React to messages
  • Participate in polls
  • Stay informed about league activities

Members cannot:

  • Create posts in the main group
  • Spam reactions or messages
  • Share inappropriate content

Representatives

Representatives can post:

  • Team announcements
  • Outreach opportunities
  • Scrimmages and events
  • Resources and workshops
  • Team accomplishments

Representatives should:

  • Verify information before posting
  • Keep messages clear and concise
  • Represent their team respectfully

Community Values

  • Gracious Professionalism
  • Collaboration
  • Learning
  • Innovation
  • Friendly Competition

Goal: Keep Agnesi Hub useful, positive, and welcoming for every team.

5 Commonly Asked Questions

Who can join Agnesi Hub?

Students, mentors, and coaches from Agnesi League FTC teams may join.

Why can't members post messages?

To keep the feed organized, focused, and useful for everyone. Members can still react, participate in polls, and stay informed.

How many representatives can each team have?

Each team should have one active representative at a time.

Can our team change representatives?

Yes. Teams can update their representative if needed.

What should representatives post?

Representatives should post information that benefits the Agnesi League community, such as events, outreach, workshops, resources, scrimmages, and team celebrations.

Can coaches and mentors join?

Yes. Students, coaches, and mentors are all welcome.
